Privacy

Privacy policy

Dino Walk collects no personal data. This page describes what the app does today.

Last updated 20 November 2026.

No personal data

Dino Walk does not create accounts, and it does not collect, store, or share personal information. There is no analytics, advertising, or tracking in the app.

Works offline

Dinosaur facts and 3D models are bundled with the app. You can choose a dinosaur, read its facts, and open the walk without a network connection.

If you tap a source link on a fact card, your device may open that museum’s webpage. That is something you choose to do, and it is not required to use the app.

World sensing, on your device

On Apple Vision Pro, Dino Walk uses world sensing only on-device to find the floor and place a life-size dinosaur in your room. Spatial data is not sent off the device, and it is not used to identify you.

When visionOS asks for permission, the app explains: “Dino Walk uses world sensing to place life-size dinosaurs on the floor of your room.”
