Life-size dinosaurs, walking through your room.

Dino Walk places a dinosaur at true scale in your real space. Watch it walk across your floor, then read facts about it — each one taken from a museum source and shown with a link.

How it works

Pick a dinosaur. Read the facts. Open the walk.

  1. Choose a dinosaur

    Browse six well-known dinosaurs in the Dino Walk window, from Tyrannosaurus rex to Parasaurolophus.

  2. Read sourced facts

    Every claim is taken from a museum source shown on the card. The facts are bundled in the app, so they work offline.

  3. Walk it through your room

    On Apple Vision Pro, open the walk. Dino Walk uses world sensing only on your device to place a life-size dinosaur on the floor, then walks it through your space.
