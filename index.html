Dino Walk

A 12-metre T. rex, walking across your floor.

On Apple Vision Pro, the dinosaur is placed at the length listed by the Natural History Museum, London. Each fact in the app cites its source.

Coming soon to Apple Vision Pro

What it does

  1. Pick a dinosaur

    Six animals are in the app: Tyrannosaurus rex, Triceratops, Stegosaurus, Brachiosaurus, Velociraptor, and Parasaurolophus.

  2. Read the museum notes

    Every claim is copied from the Natural History Museum, London, or the American Museum of Natural History. The card links to the page it came from. The notes are bundled in the app, so they work offline.

  3. Open the walk

    The app uses world sensing only on the device to find the floor, then places the dinosaur at life size and walks it through the room.
